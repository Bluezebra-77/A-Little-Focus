Install the app on your phone

iPhone: open this website in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen and choose Open as Web App if offered. Android: open in Chrome and use Install app or Add to Home screen in the menu.

Works offline after the first visit. Photos stay in this device/browser. Safari and a Home Screen app may have separate storage: export and restore a full backup when moving between them.

Your challenge settings

Changing the date rebuilds the seasonal order and keeps completions attached to their prompts. Your order is shuffled once, then saved on this browser. Today’s brief stays fixed when you reopen or refresh.

Separate image storage still uses device space. Export a full backup to Files or iCloud Drive for independent protection; Safari recovery copies are not cloud backups.

Daily recovery

Up to seven daily snapshots. Photo bytes are shared between snapshots to avoid seven duplicate photo collections. These copies are on this device; export a full backup for independent protection.

Full backups include your prompts, order, completion progress and all compressed photos. Save an independent copy to Files or iCloud Drive.

Original composition sketch • an illustrative starting point, not a real photograph or an image to submit as your own.

Your photograph

One saved photo per theme, shared across pace choices. Choose a screenshot or photo from your phone. Saved copies are at most 1,600 pixels and 200 KB, with separate small gallery thumbnails. Your original is untouched. Photos and thumbnails use separate image storage; progress and recovery contain references to them. Full exported backups contain the actual images. Nothing is uploaded to GitHub.

How to take it

A phone is fine: use the main camera, tap to focus and adjust exposure. With a camera, use a close-focusing lens for details; a light box or window works for small arrangements. Apertures are starting points, not rules.

Your photographic year

Every photo belongs to its theme. Tap a card to return to its brief. Switching pace keeps your collection.

About the images and copyright

All composition sketches are drawn specifically for this site from basic shapes, with no stock images, copied photographs, artist references or branded artwork. Use them as a starting point and make your own photographs. They are simplified visual guides rather than literal previews of every subject.

For your own pictures, avoid reproducing protected paintings, photographs or printed designs as the main subject without permission. Use plain materials or your own artwork. Copyright also depends on how a work is used; this challenge cannot guarantee clearance for every photograph you choose to make. UK Intellectual Property Office guidance.

UK seasons guide the shuffle. No special trip or purchase is required. If a seasonal subject is unavailable, use a fallen or dried sample, a houseplant, household materials or a paper interpretation of the same brief.
